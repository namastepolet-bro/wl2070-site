§01 VISION
§ 01

Vision — What We Are Building

WealthLeap 2070 (wealthleap2070.io) is a wealth-foresight platform for the age of AGI. The site today is a seed-stage teaser: three published studies set the frame — Wealth Foresight 2070 (the project's foundational map: the leap through the AGI portal, what ceases to be money yet endures as an object, and what survives two generations — with a mathematical model), The Golden Dollar 2045 (a scenario of monetary transition), and The Mindset of Affluent Arab Families (capital-retention archetypes and a formal model of multi-generational decision-making). Languages: EN / RU / AR.

The next step is to turn that frame into a large-scale collective, iterative foresight exercise: an annual programme of 6–12 invitation-only residential summits, running from October–November 2026 to October–November 2027, whose outcome is a map of the transfer of global wealth across two generations of singularity-driven transformation. Each city in the series contributes its regional layer; between sessions the result compounds and sharpens. This is not a conference series — it is the distributed production of a single intellectual product, built by the owners and stewards of capital themselves.

§02 PRODUCTS
§ 02

The Programme's Four Products

01

The iterative foresight map

A map of global wealth transfer across two generations of singularity-driven transformation. Every session adds a regional layer: assets, jurisdictions, family architectures, points of rupture.

02

The Global Scarcity Index

Built on the model in Wealth Foresight 2070: what remains scarce and valuable on the far side of the AGI portal. Sessions provide expert regional calibration of the index.

03

The wealth-transition startup funnel

Ideas born in the sessions pass through standard filters: session ideas → selection → incubation. The programme becomes a source of deal flow that clears conventional venture screens.

04

The expert dataset for an AI steward

A structured corpus of judgements from principals, family offices and their advisers: the foundation of the memorandum for the primary core of an AI steward (ultimately, an AGI custodian) of family capital for the top 0.2% of households at the apex of the wealth pyramid, and for the service layer around them.

§03 MECHANICS
§ 03

Series Mechanics: Iterations, Not Events

Single-event invariant (identical across all cities): 70–150 guests · residential format, 5–6 nights (arrival Sunday → sessions Monday–Thursday → departure Friday/Saturday) · plenary theatre 120–150 · 3 parallel breakouts × 25–50 · gala dinner 100–120 · full board · airport transfers. Venue narrative anchors: "provenance", "futurism", "closed residence". The Muscat home base opens with a calibration event for a 50–70-guest delegation, scaling to 150.

Architecture of a single iteration (proposal)

Sun
Arrival and welcome reception; an evening "state of the map" briefing: what previous cities have accumulated.
Mon
Plenary day: the foresight frame, the regional context, and the setting of the iteration's brief.
Tue–Wed
Breakout cycles along three tracks mirroring the products: the map (regional layer) · the index (scarcity calibration) · startup ideas (generation and review); evenings follow the city's cultural protocol (the gala satellites from the §4 passports).
Thu
Synthesis plenary: consolidating the tracks into a delta; the closing gala.
Fri/Sat
Departures; a closing debrief and inter-session sprint tasks for the returning core.
Dataset
Collected across all formats: recording and labelling of judgements — with participants' consent, under the NDA confidentiality protocol.

Rhythm (proposal for discussion): the programme runs as two dense "autumn waves" (Oct–Nov 2026 and Oct–Nov 2027, within the verified windows from the dossiers, at ~1–3-week intervals within each wave) and a mid-year "bridge" (Dec 2026 – Sep 2027, at ~6–8-week intervals). The base scenario totals 10 iterations, within a 6–12 range depending on team and partner readiness.

Inter-session work (proposal): after each iteration — a 2–3-week synthesis sprint (consolidating session material into a delta of the map and index); a closed delta release to the returning core of participants; movement of startup ideas through the funnel (screening → mentor review → incubation track); dataset curation (structuring, labelling, expert verification of judgements). Each new session opens with the "current state of the map" — guests work not from a blank sheet but from the accumulated result of previous cities.

Jaipur — the Indian-dynasties and NRI-capital layer

Why this city
The only market in the world where the palaces of ruling dynasties operate as hotels for 150 guests; the historic homeland of the Marwari — the merchant-banking houses behind half of India's corporate capital: the audience literally returns to its ancestral house; JAI has direct Gulf flights, and Delhi is a 50-minute flight away.
Windows and calendar
Practically all of October (Sunday arrivals 4, 11 or 25 October; note the Dussehra processions on 20 October); November is fully closed: Diwali on 8 November (week of 2–11 excluded), wedding season from ~20 November, Pushkar 17–24 November, the post-Diwali air-quality slump.
Summit house
The full-cycle Kukas campuses Fairmont Jaipur / The Leela Palace — the primary scenario for 150; the Taj pair: Rambagh Palace (a 70–78-room block for principals) + Jai Mahal Palace; JW Marriott by the airport as the capacity reserve.
Evenings and gala
City Palace — "dinner at the Maharaja's" in the courtyards of the royal family's working residence (house protocol; a welcome by a family member possible); Alila Fort Bishangarh — a two-night "fort finale" or a buyout for 70; The Oberoi Rajvilas — a buyout residence.
Protocol
Vegetarian and Jain menus are mandatory as core lines; dry days (2 October); hierarchy and seniority in seating; late evenings are the norm; a Hindi greeting is a valued gesture.
Levers
October ahead of the wedding peak; package negotiations with Taj (Rambagh + Jai Mahal) and Accor (Raffles + Fairmont); no cash subsidies (RTDC / ICPB provide facilitation and letters of support) — India's lever is the price level itself; net + 18% GST separately.
⚠
Intimate palace inventories — capacity is question number one; ~30–33°C daytime heat — climate-controlled tenting in the budget.
WL2070 · KYO · 03WL2070_Kyoto_venue-scouting.md

Kyoto — the shinise layer

Why this city
The world capital of shinise: Japan leads in companies older than 100 and 500 years, and a large share are Kyoto family houses — for a platform about century-long wealth trajectories this is not a backdrop but the research subject itself; KIX → Kyoto in 75–80 minutes on the Haruka express, Tokyo 2h10m by shinkansen; Kansai next-gen (Osaka 30 minutes away).
Windows and calendar
4–21 October and 25 October – 8 November (Sunday 1 November is the last sensible arrival date before the peak); momiji ~15 November – 5 December is "Kyoto's ADIPEC": group rates are non-negotiable; the Jidai Matsuri on 22 October partially closes the centre; check the ICC congress calendar with the CVB; the early-October typhoon tail — keep ±2 days of flexibility.
Summit house
HOTEL THE MITSUI (~161 keys on land held by the Mitsui house since the 18th century, opposite Nijo-jo — the series' most precise narrative) / Hotel Okura (a 150-seat plenary, hospitality lineage since 1888; ⚠ parts of the product are dated) / The Westin Miyako (receiving the city's guests since 1890) / the Prince Takaragaike (~300 keys) + ICC Kyoto pairing — a "state-scale plenary day" where the Kyoto Protocol was signed / ROKU KYOTO — the "closed residence" of the Takagamine valley.
Evenings and gala
Nijo-jo — a "reception at the shogun's" via the Kyoto CVB's official MICE Plan (check castle closure days); UNESCO temples with private illumination — catalogue via the bureau; principals' annexes — Aman Kyoto / Banyan Tree (a private Noh performance) / Park Hyatt; a ryokan evening (Hiiragiya / Tawaraya — 200-year institutions) — dinner "inside a shinise".
Protocol
Meishi exchanged with both hands (double-sided EN/JP cards are the admission ticket); absolute punctuality; banquets run earlier than in Europe; EN⇄JP simultaneous interpretation is mandatory (book interpreters early); shoe protocol at temple venues.
Levers
The weak yen — Kyoto luxury in USD undercuts its Swiss and Californian peers; the Kyoto CVB subsidy up to ¥300,000 / up to 50% of costs (second-half filing opens 1 September — file first); the Prince + ICC pairing is markedly cheaper than the luxury centre; comp rooms in Japan run ~1:40–50 — budget realistically; net + 10% consumption tax + the Kyoto accommodation tax (raised from 2026) separately.
⚠
The only geography in the series with no off-season lever; in the second half of November nothing is negotiable; a Tokyo alternative (Okura Tokyo / Palace Hotel) if momiji blocks the dates.

"Provenance" from the riverside grand hotels with 150 years of history, "futurism" from Mahanakhon and One Bangkok; venue clusters even tighter than Abu Dhabi's — half the transfers happen on foot or by boat; Singapore and Hong Kong family offices are a 2–3-hour flight away.

Halal certification for Malaysian and Indonesian guests; Chinese banquet expertise (round tables, seating protocol) for Thai-Chinese families; a subdued tone on 13 and 23 October; check alcohol-ban dates against the windows.
Levers
TCEB — with Kyoto, the series' only "money on the table": financial support plus the MICE Premium Lane airport fast-track (apply 30+ days ahead; a TMVS-standard venue required); Bangkok is markedly cheaper than Abu Dhabi for comparable luxury; opening rates at new hotels; block → free function space.

§ 06

Economics and Levers

A summary of the budget levers from the dossiers (all figures indicative, "not an offer"; final numbers come only from RFPs):

The room block as the universal lever
350–900 room-nights per event; at that volume function space is typically free or heavily discounted, and complimentary rooms are market standard at ~1:25–40 (more modest in Japan, ~1:40–50).
Off-season timing
Switzerland (Oct–Nov is low season), Lisbon (shoulder season outside Web Summit), California (post-harvest November plus the dead midweek at wedding venues), Jaipur (October before the wedding peak). The exception is Kyoto: autumn is peak — offset by the weak yen and the CVB subsidy.
Actual money on the table
TCEB (Thailand — financial support plus airport fast-track; apply 30+ days ahead) and the Kyoto CVB (up to ¥300,000, up to 50% of eligible costs; second-half filing opens 1 September — file first). Other bureaus (Lisboa CB, Switzerland CIB, Visit Napa Valley / SF Travel, INPROTUR / Visit Buenos Aires, RTDC/ICPB) provide facilitation without subsidies.
Tax mechanics
Argentina — 21% IVA becomes 0% on non-resident lodging paid from abroad (class "T" invoicing); everywhere — net rates with taxes shown separately (VAT / 18% GST in India / consumption + accommodation tax in Kyoto / ~12–15% occupancy tax in California / 5% VAT in Oman).
Structural plays
Full buyouts (Capella Bangkok, Oberoi Rajvilas, Alila Fort Bishangarh, Rosewood Sand Hill, FS Calistoga) are often better value than a block plus venue hire; package negotiations within groups (Taj: Rambagh + Jai Mahal; Accor: Raffles + Fairmont; Sutton Dabbah: Alvear + Icon + Llao Llao); USD prepayment in Argentina is worth a real 5–10% discount; opening rates at new hotels (Ritz-Carlton One Bangkok).
The series effect (proposal)
A 10-iteration programme means 3,500–9,000 hotel nights a year; serial-client status and the global-sales channels of the chains (Marriott, Accor, Hyatt, Four Seasons, MO) unlock a negotiating tier unavailable to a one-off event.
